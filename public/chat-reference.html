Can you explain how derivatives help us understand changing quantities?
MTH103 · AssistantDerivatives measure the rate at which one quantity changes in relation to another. A useful way to picture this is the slope of a curve: a steep slope means the quantity is changing quickly, while a flatter slope means the change is slower.
